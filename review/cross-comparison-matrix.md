# Cross-Comparison Matrix

## Purpose

This matrix compares the ten initial historical and contemporary precedent candidates against the six novelty criteria used in the Subjectivity Intersection Ontology (SIO) review program.

The table is provisional. It summarizes the current public reviews and must be revised whenever stronger primary-text evidence, a better interpretation, or an exact counterexample is identified.

## Status symbols

- **Y** — currently supported by the reviewed framework
- **P** — partial, ambiguous, or interpretation-dependent support
- **N** — currently not supported
- **U** — unresolved because the necessary primary-text or empirical evidence has not yet been established

## Criteria

- **N1 — Joint generation:** C is generated through the active relation of differentiated poles A and B.
- **N2 — History irreducibility:** C depends constitutively on the specific interaction history of A and B.
- **N3 — Third subjectivity:** C is an irreducible subject or locus of experience, not merely a field, concept, process, meaning, institution, or observer description.
- **N4 — Constitutive mediation:** C is not only an outcome of A–B interaction but also helps constitute the continuing A–B relation.
- **N5 — Return action:** C acts back upon and transforms both A and B.
- **N6 — Cross-scale recurrence:** The same generative structure recurs invariantly across relevant levels or scales.

## Important interpretation rule

The SIO row does **not** mean that all six criteria have already been demonstrated. It records the intended structure of the research program. The historical rows record the present assessment of what each comparison framework appears to establish.

The matrix must therefore not be read as:

> SIO is proven while its predecessors are incomplete.

It should be read as:

> SIO explicitly targets a conjunction of six conditions that no reviewed predecessor has yet been shown to satisfy in full.

## Strongest near-matches

### Analytic third

The analytic third currently appears to be the closest pair-specific relational precedent. It strongly supports joint generation, interaction history, constitutive mediation, and return transformation. The decisive unresolved issue is whether the third is literally an irreducible subject of experience or a clinical-relational field, shared unconscious organization, symbolic position, or emergent perspective.

### Participatory sense-making

Participatory sense-making provides the strongest dynamical precedent for jointly generated interactional autonomy and return influence upon both participants. The central unresolved issue is whether autonomous interaction is itself subjective or experiential.

### Whitehead

Whitehead provides the strongest precedent for genuinely emergent subjectivity. The principal mismatch is that an actual occasion ordinarily arises through temporal many-to-one concrescence from objectified predecessors, rather than through a simultaneous, pair-specific closure among two active subjects and a generated third.

### Christian Trinitarian ontology

Trinitarian ontology provides the strongest precedent for a genuine irreducible third Person within ontological unity. The unresolved issue is whether the third is generated as the subjectivity of an intersection between differentiated perspectives, rather than eternally constituted through procession and relation.

### Nishida and Merleau-Ponty

Both provide exceptionally strong accounts of relational constitution, mediation, mutual transformation, and non-dual structure. The unresolved issue is whether place, world, flesh, chiasm, or the between is itself an irreducible subjectivity.

## Current cross-tradition conclusion

> **No reviewed framework has yet been shown to satisfy N1–N6 together in the specific SIO sense.**

This is a defeasible review conclusion, not a universal proof of historical originality.

## Defeat condition

The conclusion must be revised or withdrawn if one historical or contemporary framework is shown, through adequate primary evidence, to establish all of the following together:

1. differentiated active subjective poles A and B;
2. pair-specific joint generation of C;
3. constitutive dependence on A–B interaction history;
4. irreducible subjective or experiential status of C;
5. C as constitutive mediator of the continuing A–B relation;
6. return action by C upon both A and B;
7. recurrence of the same generative structure across relevant scales.
