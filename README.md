## Mission

Subjectivity Intersection Ontology is developed as part of the Subjectivity Intersection Emergence Project (SIEP), an open research initiative dedicated to advancing a unified understanding of subjectivity across philosophy, mathematics, science, and artificial intelligence through transparent, collaborative, and evidence-conscious research.

The long-term goal is to establish a permanent public resource that belongs not to a single researcher, but to humanity.
