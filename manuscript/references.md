# References

The bibliography distinguishes texts and works directly analyzed in the comparison from interpretive, methodological, and interdisciplinary literature. For ancient and classical texts, citations in the manuscript use conventional divisions—fragment, book, chapter, section, or sūtra—where available, so that passages can be located across editions and translations. “DK” refers to the numbering in Diels and Kranz (1951–1952).
