Appendix A

Open Research Methodology and Repository Documentation

## A.1 Purpose

This appendix explains the research process through which the present manuscript on Subjectivity Intersection Ontology (SIO) was drafted, revised, and made publicly available.

The comparative methodology, N1–N6, and the structural assessment procedure are defined in Chapter 3 and are not restated here. This appendix concerns version control, the distinction between working drafts and the current manuscript, the use of public issues to document research questions, and the use of AI-assisted tools.

The public repository is not evidence supporting the conclusions of this paper. It is a record of the research process.

## A.2 Open Development

The manuscript was developed not as a single document disclosed only upon completion, but as a version-controlled work with a recorded history of change. Initial definitions, historical comparisons, evaluation criteria, chapter drafts, and the integrated manuscript were documented incrementally in a public repository.

This history allows readers to trace how initial definitions, the comparative methodology, and individual assessments were revised before reaching their current form.

The public record does not imply that the comparative criteria were preregistered before the research began. It shows that the methodology itself underwent examination and revision and that its current definition is stated explicitly in the manuscript.

## A.3 Repository Structure

This study used GitHub to make the manuscript, drafts, comparative materials, and research questions publicly available under version control. The research repository is available at:

[https://github.com/SIEL-Research/Subjectivity-Intersection-Ontology](https://github.com/SIEL-Research/Subjectivity-Intersection-Ontology)

The repository distinguishes the current integrated manuscript from materials belonging to its development.

| Location | Contents | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [`manuscript/`](https://github.com/SIEL-Research/Subjectivity-Intersection-Ontology/tree/main/manuscript) | Abstract, Chapters 1–7, and Appendix A | Current author-approved manuscript |
| [`drafts/`](https://github.com/SIEL-Research/Subjectivity-Intersection-Ontology/tree/main/drafts) | Chapter drafts, initial definitions, and earlier structures | Working history and source archive |
| [`comparisons/`](https://github.com/SIEL-Research/Subjectivity-Intersection-Ontology/tree/main/comparisons) | Initial comparisons of individual theories | Supplementary materials documenting the development of the historical comparison |
| [`review/`](https://github.com/SIEL-Research/Subjectivity-Intersection-Ontology/tree/main/review) | Search strategies, selection criteria, comparison matrices, and reviews of potential weaknesses | Records of methodological development and internal audit |
| [Public issues](https://github.com/SIEL-Research/Subjectivity-Intersection-Ontology/issues) | Comparison candidates, possible counterexamples, unresolved questions, and revision tasks | Public record of research questions and tasks |
| [Revision history](https://github.com/SIEL-Research/Subjectivity-Intersection-Ontology/commits/main/) | Content and sequence of file changes | Version history and provenance information |

For the current content of the paper, `manuscript/` is authoritative. Materials in `drafts/`, `comparisons/`, `review/`, and the public issues may contain definitions, methods, or provisional assessments that were subsequently revised or withdrawn. They do not replace the final manuscript; they preserve its developmental history.

The repository serves as a supplementary record through which the provenance of individual judgments may be examined.

## A.4 AI Assistance and Authorial Responsibility

AI-assisted tools were used during the development of this manuscript for drafting, Japanese–English translation, stylistic editing, organization of chapter structure, terminology consistency checks, and repository integration.

Outputs from AI-assisted tools were not treated as primary sources, specialist scholarship, or independent evidence for philosophical or historical claims. The ontological definition of SIO, the selection of comparison targets, the final meanings of N1–N6, the individual assessments, the limits placed upon the claims, and the final wording were reviewed and approved by the author.

AI assistance does not transfer authorial responsibility. Responsibility for the accuracy of the sources, historical interpretations, comparative assessments, translations, and final manuscript remains with the author. If the journal of submission requires a separate disclosure of AI use, an additional statement will be provided in accordance with that policy.

## A.5 Public Review and Formal Peer Review

Public issues and the revision history were used to document comparison candidates, possible counterexamples, methodological questions, and revision tasks. The existence of an issue, the number of comments it receives, or its open or closed status does not constitute scholarly agreement or peer-review approval.

Public discussion does not replace formal peer review. Its function is to make the development of the manuscript traceable and to allow readers or reviewers to examine the provenance of particular judgments when necessary. Public review provides transparency concerning the research process; formal peer review provides a distinct process of expert and institutional evaluation.

## A.6 Limits of Transparency

Version control makes it possible to retrieve a particular version of the manuscript, compare passages before and after revision, and inspect the consistency among the criteria in Chapter 3, the assessments in Chapter 4, and the integrated results in Chapter 5. Transparency, however, does not guarantee correctness. Researchers consulting the same materials may reach different interpretations or structural assessments.

Because the `main` branch is updated over time, it is not an immutable object of citation. Criticism or citation of a specific version should identify a complete commit SHA, release tag, or permanently archived version.

Repository timestamps and revision history establish the order of documentation within this repository. They do not demonstrate intellectual priority, independent discovery, or absolute historical novelty.

## A.7 Academic Status

The research repository preserves the distinction between the current manuscript and earlier versions, the revision history leading from drafts to the integrated manuscript, comparative materials, public issues, and unresolved questions. These records support the provenance, transparency, and auditability of the research process.

The repository is not independent evidence for the ontological validity of SIO, its structural distinguishability within the examined historical comparison set, or the empirical reality of third subjectivity. The conclusions of this paper must be evaluated on the basis of the definitions, primary sources, interpretations, comparative methodology, and limitations presented in the main text.

The purpose of this appendix is not to strengthen the case for the validity of SIO, but to make the research process underlying the manuscript publicly traceable and auditable. The transparency documented here should be distinguished from the philosophical claims advanced in the main text.
