# Cosmopsychism and Subjectivity Intersection Ontology

## Status

**Provisional verdict:**

> **STRONG SUBJECTIVITY-FIRST AND WHOLE–PART PRECEDENT, NOT AN EXACT MATCH ON CURRENT EVIDENCE**

This review is part of the systematic historical audit of possible precedents to Subjectivity Intersection Ontology (SIO). The question is not whether cosmopsychism is broadly compatible with SIO, but whether any cosmopsychist model satisfies all six novelty criteria defined by this project.

## Review target

This document audits contemporary cosmopsychism and closely related views, including:

- priority cosmopsychism;
- constitutive cosmopsychism;
- emergent cosmopsychism;
- Russellian-monist variants;
- accounts of cosmic consciousness and cosmic subjectivity;
- combination and decombination models;
- whole–part accounts of individual minds;
- top-down grounding and constitutive dependence.

## Core cosmopsychist structure

Cosmopsychism places consciousness or subjectivity at the level of the cosmos as a whole. Individual subjects are then explained through a relation of grounding, constitution, restriction, localization, derivation, fragmentation, or decombination from cosmic subjectivity.

A minimal schematic form is:

```text
cosmic subject / cosmic consciousness
                ↓
      individual subjects or minds
```

This is importantly different from the central SIO proposal:

```text
differentiated subject A + differentiated subject B
                         ↓
          pair-specific intersection subject C
                         ↓
             return transformation of A and B
```

The first structure begins from an already unified cosmic subject. The second proposes a subject generated through the intersection of differentiated subjectivities.

## Why cosmopsychism is a serious precedent

Cosmopsychism is highly relevant because it:

- treats subjectivity as ontologically fundamental rather than derivative from non-experiential matter;
- rejects the assumption that isolated individual subjects are the ultimate units of reality;
- attributes genuine subjectivity to a larger whole;
- attempts to explain local subjects through relations internal to a more comprehensive subject;
- raises explicit questions about how one subject can contain, constitute, ground, or generate others;
- offers possible recursive whole–part structures across levels.

It therefore challenges any overly broad claim that SIO is the first ontology to place subjectivity at multiple scales or to treat larger-scale subjectivity as metaphysically real.

## Criterion-by-criterion audit

### N1 — Joint generation

**Provisional assessment: not established.**

Most cosmopsychist views do not begin with two differentiated active subjects jointly generating a third. They begin with a cosmic subject or cosmic consciousness that is ontologically prior to individual minds.

Even where relations among local subjects contribute to larger-scale organization, the cosmic subject is usually not defined as pair-specifically generated by those subjects.

Cosmopsychism may therefore support dependence across scales without supporting joint generation in the SIO sense.

### N2 — History irreducibility

**Provisional assessment: weak or model-dependent.**

Cosmopsychist theories are usually formulated as synchronic grounding or constitution theories. The existence of the cosmic subject does not normally depend on the unique interaction history of a particular pair of local subjects.

Some developmental or emergent versions may introduce temporal history, but this is not a generic feature of cosmopsychism and must be demonstrated model by model.

### N3 — Irreducible third subjectivity

**Provisional assessment: strongly supported in one sense, not in the required sense.**

Cosmopsychism explicitly posits a real subject at the cosmic level. This is stronger than theories that posit only a field, system, relation, or collective process.

However, the cosmic subject is not normally a third subjectivity generated between two differentiated subjective perspectives. It is an encompassing or prior subject.

Thus cosmopsychism supports irreducible higher-order subjectivity but does not yet support **intersection-generated** third subjectivity.

### N4 — Constitutive mediation

**Provisional assessment: partial support.**

The cosmic subject may be constitutive of individual subjects, and individual subjects may be intelligible only through their relation to the cosmic whole. This gives cosmopsychism a strong whole–part constitutive structure.

But SIO requires a relational unity generated by A and B that also constitutes the A–B relation. Cosmopsychist constitution is usually asymmetric or top-down rather than a pair-generated mediating closure.

### N5 — Return action

**Provisional assessment: partial and model-dependent.**

If the cosmic subject grounds, constrains, or organizes individual subjects, then a form of downward determination may be present.

However, SIO requires a more specific structure:

```text
A and B jointly generate C;
C acts back upon A and B.
```

In cosmopsychism, the cosmic subject generally does not arise from the pair whose states it constrains. Downward constitution alone therefore does not establish SIO-style return action.

### N6 — Recursive recurrence

**Provisional assessment: potentially strong, not yet demonstrated in invariant form.**

Cosmopsychism naturally invites multilevel models in which subjects are related as wholes and parts. This may support recurrence across cosmic, biological, social, or local levels.

Yet recurrence of subjectivity at multiple levels is not sufficient. The same exact relational form must recur, including joint generation, pair specificity, irreducibility, constitutive mediation, and return action.

No such invariant six-criterion recurrence has yet been established.

## Strongest apparent overlap

The strongest overlap lies in the rejection of isolated-subject atomism.

Both cosmopsychism and SIO allow that:

- subjectivity may exist at more than one scale;
- larger-scale subjectivity may be ontologically real;
- individual subjects may be internally related to a more comprehensive subjective structure;
- subjectivity cannot be fully explained as an accidental property of otherwise non-subjective matter.

This overlap is philosophically important and must be acknowledged explicitly.

## Decisive difference

The decisive difference is generative direction and relational specificity.

```text
Cosmopsychism:
cosmic subject → individual subjects

SIO:
subject A + subject B → intersection subject C
C → transformed A and B
```

Cosmopsychism primarily addresses how many local subjects relate to one cosmic subject.

SIO addresses how a new subjectivity can arise through the relation of differentiated subjects while remaining irreducible to both and returning causal or constitutive influence to them.

## Combination and decombination

Cosmopsychism is often motivated partly by the combination problem for panpsychism. Instead of asking how many micro-subjects combine into a macro-subject, cosmopsychism begins with cosmic consciousness and must explain how local subjects arise from it.

This produces a decombination problem:

- How can one cosmic subject ground or contain many distinct subjects?
- How are their perspectives bounded?
- How can one subject divide into genuinely distinct centers without either collapsing their independence or fragmenting the cosmic unity?

These questions are highly relevant to SIO, but they are not identical to the problem of intersection-generated subjectivity.

## Possible exact-match routes

A cosmopsychist theory could approach an exact match if it established all of the following:

1. Two differentiated local subjects jointly generate a third subjectivity.
2. The third is pair-specific and history-dependent.
3. The third is irreducible to the cosmic subject considered independently of the pair.
4. The third constitutes the ongoing relation between the pair.
5. The third acts back upon both generating subjects.
6. The same generative structure recurs invariantly across scales.

No reviewed cosmopsychist position has yet been shown to satisfy all six conditions.

## Current conclusion

Cosmopsychism is a major precedent for subjectivity-first metaphysics, non-atomistic theories of mind, real higher-order subjectivity, and multilevel whole–part constitution.

It is not, on current evidence, an exact precedent for Subjectivity Intersection Ontology.

The unresolved issue is not whether cosmopsychism admits a higher-order subject. It does. The unresolved issue is whether that subject is generated by the intersection of differentiated subjects in a pair-specific, history-irreducible, recursively closed structure.

Accordingly, the current verdict remains:

> **STRONG SUBJECTIVITY-FIRST AND WHOLE–PART PRECEDENT, NOT AN EXACT MATCH ON CURRENT EVIDENCE**

## Required primary-source audit

Future revisions should separately examine:

- priority and constitutive cosmopsychism;
- Philip Goff's formulations of cosmopsychism;
- Itay Shani on cosmic consciousness;
- Sam Coleman on subject combination and subject-summing;
- Hedda Hassel Mørch on combination, constitution, and causal structure;
- Luke Roelofs on collective and composite consciousness;
- Russellian monism and its relation to cosmopsychism;
- emergent and evolutionary cosmopsychist variants;
- the decombination problem;
- any model attributing pair-generated subjectivity to relations among local subjects.

## Evidence rule

Do not equate cosmic consciousness, higher-order subjectivity, downward grounding, whole–part constitution, or multilevel mentality with intersection subjectivity. An exact-match verdict requires explicit evidence for all six novelty criteria, including pair-specific joint generation and return action.
